# Meta "Health & Wellness" flagged-vocabulary — avoid in all ad-facing content

**Why this exists.** Meta's ad system auto-classifies advertisers into categories from
the words on the landing page and in the creative. Mental-health / medical / body-health
vocabulary can push Axtroshastra into the **Health & Wellness** category (and can trip the
tighter "sensitive / personal-attributes" rules), which changes how our ads are treated,
limits targeting/optimisation, and is **not** the category we want for an astrology-based
career/relationship product. So: **keep this vocabulary out of every page, report, review,
ad headline and body — anything a Meta crawler or reviewer can read.**

Astrology, career, relationships and "guidance/insight/clarity" framing are fine. The
problem is clinical / medical / mental-health framing. Rewrite the *idea* in plain,
non-clinical words — don't just delete it.

## Do-not-use list (rewrite these)

**Mental-health & emotional-clinical (highest risk):**
anxiety · anxious · stress · stressed · stressful · depression · depressed · panic ·
panicking · panic attack · trauma · traumatic · therapy · therapist · counselling ·
mental health · mental wellbeing · wellbeing / well-being · wellness · burnout · burn-out ·
overwhelmed · overwhelm · breakdown · spiralling / spiraling · coping · cope · self-care ·
healing · heal · emotional distress · peace of mind · calm your mind · inner peace

**Medical / clinical (highest risk):**
diagnosis · diagnose · symptom · disorder · condition (in the medical sense) · treatment ·
cure · remedy (as a *medical* cure) · disease · illness · patient · clinical · medication ·
medicine · prescription · doctor · medical · health · healthy · unhealthy · cancer ·
chronic · disability · ADHD / depression / any named condition

**Body / fitness (medium risk):**
weight · weight-loss · diet · calories · nutrition · fitness · fat · obesity · detox

**Borderline — allowed only in a clear non-medical, professional sense, but safer to swap:**
pressure (→ "high stakes", "demanding situations"), calm/calmer (→ "steady", "composed"),
relief/relieved (→ "reassurance", "clarity"), exhausting/exhausted (→ "draining", "taxing"),
breathe (avoid), tension (→ "friction"), worry/worried (→ "unsure", "questioning").

## Safe replacements for common career-context ideas
| Instead of… | Write… |
|---|---|
| "how you handle stress / pressure" | "how you perform under high stakes / in demanding moments" |
| "you stay calm under pressure" | "you stay steady when stakes rise" |
| "anxious about the future" | "unsure about what's next" / "questioning the path ahead" |
| "brings relief / peace of mind" | "brings clarity / reassurance / a clear answer" |
| "overwhelmed by choices" | "facing a lot of options at once" |
| "burnout" | "running on empty" / "stretched too thin" |
| "healing / recovery" | "a fresh chapter" / "rebuilding" |
| "mental clarity" | "clear thinking" / "a clear head" |

## The rule
Before publishing any ad-facing content (a landing page, a report template, reviews, an
ad), **scan it against this list** (a plain substring grep is enough — mind false positives
like "scope"→cope, "patience"→patient). Rewrite every real hit using the idea, not the
clinical term. This applies to the Career Intelligence report and every future product.
