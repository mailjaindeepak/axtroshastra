## 4. Pages 4 to 8: the five work-from-home cards (3 points each)
**Page 4: Choosing your corner** (R, general guidance only)
1. North, east or north-east of the home. "Sources most often prefer these sides for a workspace. This report cannot see where your desk is, so treat this as a guide." (R)
2. A solid wall behind you. "It gives a feeling of support when you work alone." (R)
3. Not against a toilet or kitchen wall. "Avoid putting your back to either." (R)

**Page 5: Bedroom as office** (R for the advice to avoid it; the fixes are NEW)
1. Work and rest are traditionally kept apart. "If the bedroom has to double as an office, keep the work area as far from the bed as you can." (R for the principle)
2. Screen the bed during work. "A screen, a shelf or even a curtain does the job." (NEW)
3. Keep the laptop off the bed. "Working in bed blurs work and rest." (S)

**Page 6: Light and desk setup** (NEW: the research is silent on light for home desks. **Please confirm or cut the whole card.**)

3. Screen away from a mirror. "A mirror facing the desk is traditionally avoided." (R for the mirror, from the Student sources; the screen wording is NEW)

**Page 7: A clear desk and a clear room** (NEW, practical, same idea as the approved Student and Aspirant desk lines)

3. Tidy cables. "Tie them up and keep chargers in one place." (NEW)
