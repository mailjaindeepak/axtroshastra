# Vastu Career Report: direction and numerology research (20 Nov 2026)

Purpose: the findings behind the draft direction matrix and number tables in `docs/vastu-career-report-spec.md`, so later sessions do not repeat the research. Nothing here is final until the owner approves it cell by cell.

Caveat: every source is a real-estate, interior-design or numerology site, not a classical text. They describe traditional beliefs, they are not scientific evidence, and they disagree in places. The owner should confirm against the classical or trusted source they use. Searches were run on 20 Nov 2026.

## 2. Mulank mappings
- Ruling planets (consistent across sources): 1 Sun, 2 Moon, 3 Jupiter, 4 Rahu, 5 Mercury, 6 Venus, 7 Ketu, 8 Saturn, 9 Mars. Mulank is the birth day reduced to one digit.
- Direction: no source gave a full Mulank-to-direction table; only Mulank 1 (Sun, east) was stated directly. The derived route is Mulank to ruling planet to that planet's classical direction: Sun east, Venus south-east, Mars south, Rahu south-west, Saturn west, Moon north-west, Mercury north, Jupiter north-east. Ketu is unsettled: north-west in one scheme, north-east in another, unassigned in a third. This affects Mulank 7.
- A planet's direction is a zone, not necessarily a facing direction, and can contradict Vastu (Mulank 9, Mars, south versus "avoid facing south"). Rule B in the spec makes Vastu win.
- Colours (2 to 8 mostly from a single numerologist, so no consensus): 1 gold, orange, yellow, or red and maroon; 2 white, cream, silver, light blue; 3 yellow shades; 4 white, yellow, green; 5 green shades; 6 white and off-white; 7 white, yellow, red; 8 white, yellow, green; 9 red, pink, maroon, orange. Strong reds and oranges clash with the light-colour advice for study desks, so show them as accents.
- A search for a general "birth number to lucky direction" table only surfaced Feng Shui Kua numbers, which is a different system and is not used here.
