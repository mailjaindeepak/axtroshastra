# Diwali report research, round 1 (raw)

Raw output of the multi-agent research run (19 Oct 2026, 23 agents). Every claim carries its source. "claimed" = the site's own number. Fact-check verdicts at the bottom; trust those over the team text where they disagree.

## Team A - Competitor reports

Across 10 or more astrology and puja sites checked on 19 Oct 2026, nobody sells a report made for Diwali: no Diwali-to-Diwali money year, no Samvat (Hindu new year) report, and no puja time for the buyer's own city inside a paid report. At festivals the big sites just run sales on their usual reports. AstroSage's Diwali 2025 sale had Brihat ₹399 and Raj Yoga and Yearbook at ₹299 each. Puja apps sell group Lakshmi pujas done in the buyer's name for ₹799-₹3,500. Every buyer expects a few basics: birth date, time and place; money, career and health sections; month-by-month timing; remedies; and fast delivery as a PDF on WhatsApp. I spot-checked 3 key claims myself (AstroTalk Dhan Yog, the GaneshaSpeaks 2027 Highlights report, the AstroSage Diwali 2025 sale) and all 3 matched.

### Recommendations

- Make the report run from Diwali 2026 to Diwali 2027 (Samvat 2083), not Jan-Dec. No competitor does this.
- Ask for the buyer's current city as well as birth place, so the report can print their own Lakshmi Puja time. Check the times against Drik Panchang, which differs from AstroSage by 1 minute.
- Copy the standard form: name, gender, birth date, time (with an 'I don't know' option), place, WhatsApp number, language. Add 1 optional free-text question; GaneshaSpeaks shows buyers value this. Gotra is optional, for a personal touch.
- Include month-by-month money windows, career, health, remedies, and a short 'money blueprint' (lucky days and directions). These are basics buyers expect.
- Add an optional new-year / account-book section for business families (Gujarati, Marwari, Jain).
- Launch price ₹299-₹499, with sales closing before Lakshmi Puja on 8 Nov 2026. Consider a family plan (2-4 people), the way Sri Mandir prices by group size.
- Stand apart on trust: a real public sample, delivery-time and refund promises we can keep, and no fake struck-out prices. Fix the WhatsApp delivery gap (no 'sent' records since 28 Sep) before launch.

## Team B - What people ask around Diwali

Around Diwali, people mostly ask practical questions: which day it falls on, what the puja time is for their city, and when to buy gold. Horoscope questions come second, and the strongest of those is "how will my money be from this Diwali to next Diwali". Most of this content is in Hindi and is written once per sign, so it is the same for everyone. Nobody seems to have claimed "Diwali 2026 horoscope" searches yet. The biggest complaints about paid reports are that two people get the same text, the PDF is long and padded, the dates are wrong, and the seller pushes fear-based upsells. A birth-chart report can honestly cover a personal 12-month money and career outlook plus exact city timings. It cannot honestly give stock tips, gold prices or guaranteed results.

### Findings

- **[high]** Diwali (Lakshmi Puja) 2026 is on Sunday 8 Nov. Sites agree on the day but disagree on the puja time window by up to about 1 hour for the same city. For New Delhi: SmartPuja says 5:32-7:28 PM, Shubh Panchang says 6:29-8:29 PM and Dekho Panchang says 5:47-7:13 PM. I spot-checked this myself, so it is confirmed. Exact city timings checked against Drik Panchang are therefore real value.

### Recommendations

- Main promise: 'Your money year: Diwali 2026 to Diwali 2027', with month-by-month good and careful periods and the reason from the buyer's chart.
- Inputs to ask for: name, birth date, birth time, birth place, how sure they are of the birth time (lower how firmly we predict if unsure), current city (for puja timings), main focus (job / business / money / family), and optionally one question in their own words. Also ask for community or new-year style (Diwali, Bestu Varas, Jain, Marathi Padwa) so we can show the right new-year timings.
- Page 1 shows the buyer's own details and chart facts (rising sign, moon sign, birth star, and current planet period, meaning which planet 'rules' their life right now) so it never looks like a generic '12 signs' article.
- Include city-exact Lakshmi Puja, Dhanteras and chopda pujan times, checked against Drik Panchang before launch. Wrong dates are a named complaint.
- Add a block that stops reports going out with the same text: if the AI text fails, re-run it or hold the report. Never send the standard backup text quietly. This is the exact failure in Career Intelligence and the top complaint against competitors.
- Keep it short, about 8 to 12 pages. No fear words (no dosha panic; a dosha is a 'bad mark' in the chart), no paid remedies, no stock or gold tips. Use simple free remedies only.
- Keep 'what to buy on Dhanteras by sign' as a small box at most. Demand for it is weak.
- Ship the Hindi version alongside English. Put the Diwali-to-Diwali wording and 'kab hai / shubh muhurat' terms in the landing page copy.

## Team C - New year communities and Diwali traditions

For four groups, Gujaratis, Marwaris (and other Rajasthani business families), Jains and Newars, the days after Diwali are a real new year, and for traders the new account books open at the Diwali puja. In 2026 Lakshmi Puja is Sun 8 Nov, Mon 9 Nov is an empty gap day, and the new year (Vikram Samvat 2083) is Tue 10 Nov. For Tamil Nadu and Karnataka, Diwali is a pre-dawn oil-bath and family festival, not a new year. Maharashtra keeps all five days, and Padwa there has a husband-wife side and a new-account-books side. Puja times change by city by more than an hour, so the report must calculate them from the buyer's current city. We should not market to Sikhs, and the Jain wording needs care.

### Findings

- **[high]** I checked the 2026 five-day calendar against Drik Panchang myself. Thu 5 Nov: Vasu Baras. Fri 6 Nov: Dhanteras. Sat 7 Nov: Kali Chaudas (Gujarat). Sun 8 Nov: Naraka Chaturdashi (the pre-dawn oil-bath day), Tamil Deepavali, Lakshmi Puja, Chopda/Sharda Puja (blessing the new account books) and Kali Puja. Mon 9 Nov: gap day (only 'Diwali Snan, Devpuja' is listed). Tue 10 Nov: Govardhan Puja, Annakut, Bali Pratipada and Gujarati New Year. Wed 11 Nov: Bhai Dooj / Bhau Beej. All four researchers agree with this.  
  Sources: https://www.drikpanchang.com/diwali/diwali-puja-calendar.html?year=2026
- **[high]** Puja times depend on the city. I checked Bengaluru on Drik Panchang myself: Lakshmi Puja is 6:17 to 8:20 PM. Delhi is 5:54 to 7:50 PM and Kolkata is 5:20 to 7:18 PM (Researcher 4, from Drik Panchang). The new-moon day (Amavasya) runs from 11:27 AM on 8 Nov to 12:31 PM on 9 Nov. DekhoPanchang gives shorter windows, because it counts the evening window as sunset plus 96 minutes. So the report has to use one method, Drik Panchang's, and work the times out from the buyer's city.  
  Sources: https://www.drikpanchang.com/festivals/lakshmipuja/festivals-lakshmipuja-timings.html?geoname-id=1277333&year=2026, https://dekhopanchang.com/en/festivals/diwali
- **[high]** I checked Wikipedia myself. The Jain year starts the day after Diwali, Jain traders start new account books then, and most Jains avoid firecrackers because of non-violence (ahimsa). Diwali marks the day Mahavir reached nirvana (final release from rebirth). It is not a Lakshmi festival for Jains. Because of the 2026 gap day, the exact Jain new-year date (9 or 10 Nov) is unconfirmed.  
  Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diwali_(Jainism), https://www.reonline.org.uk/festivals-calendar/mahavir-nirvani/
- **[medium]** Gujarati and Marwari traders open their new account books (chopda / bahi-khata) at the Diwali puja on 8 Nov. They write 'Shubh' (good luck) and 'Labh' (profit) in them. They reopen their shops on Labh Panchami, Sat 14 Nov, which is seen as a lucky day to start new ventures. Their prayers are about profit, smooth deals and removing obstacles. The sources are mostly Hindutone plus Drik Panchang; the 14 Nov date was not checked against Drik Panchang.

- **[medium]** Muhurat trading (a special one-hour stock market session for good luck) is on Sun 8 Nov. NSE says the timing 'shall be notified subsequently'. ### Recommendations

- Form: 6 required fields (name, birth date, birth time with how sure, birth place, current city, WhatsApp) plus 2 tap choices (work type, main focus). That makes 8, Baymard's limit. Everything else is optional or skipped. Do not ask for email.

- Personalisation fix (lesson from CI): give Diwali its own SECTION_SPECS entry and call narr() in the renderer. Pass work type, focus and current city into _facts_for_llm. Base the fallback text on chart facts plus the buyer's answers, never on one fixed sentence.
- Before launch, generate 5 test reports for different buyers and compare them side by side. If any paragraph outside the dates and numbers is identical, the gate fails.
- Quote the buyer's focus and work type back to them in a 'Your year at a glance' section, so the effort shows. The idea behind this, people valuing visible work (Buell & Norton), is a lab finding; test it with an A/B test that runs the form with and without the focus question for one week.
