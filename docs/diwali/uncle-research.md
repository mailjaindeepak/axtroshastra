## Central question
"Will the period after Diwali bring better fortune, money and new opportunities into my life?"

This combines three powerful purchase motivations: financial aspirations, uncertainty about the future, and the cultural significance of Diwali as a time for prosperity and new beginnings.

## Four dimensions
- **Wealth:** Will finances improve? What do traditional planetary indicators suggest?
- **Career and business:** Will new opportunities emerge?
- **Fortune:** Which upcoming periods are traditionally considered more favourable?
- **Personalised Diwali guidance:** Birth-chart interpretation, traditional remedies, auspicious dates and rituals.
