## Failures (each with exact repro)

Repro helper for any version vNN: `python3 -c "import sys;sys.path.insert(0,'tests');import test_diwali_versions as T;p,h=T.build(NN-1);print(T.chk_cards(p,h))"`.

### F1. One-month timeline segment printed as "Nov–Nov" (FIXED: re-run 2026-10-26 `-k cards` = 28 passed / 0 failed, and 0 of 28 rendered reports contain a `Mmm–Mmm` same-month range; the snapshot table above still shows the older 21 of 28 `cards` FAILs) — RENDER-A
`diwali_report.py:656` always writes `{_mon(s["from"])}–{_mon(s["to"])}` in the "Your months at a glance" legend.
When a timeline part is a single month the buyer sees "Nov–Nov · A steady new start." / "Mar–Mar · Your best time."
Repro: v05 Falguni Desai, 2003-09-17 07:52, Kolkata, T1 → `T.build(4)`, card `s-0-6`.
Fix: print one month when `from == to` (as `_mons`/`range_tile` already do).

### F2. T2/T3 + Better luck picked says "brightest months", in the report AND the free preview (v06, v13, v20) — CONTENT
Two separate sources; fixing one does not fix the other. Spec §2 rule 10: no best time with an unsure birth time.
(a) Report: `diwali_bank.py:1708` (answers for `luck`, Q2, state good) prints "Your brightest months are marked on your timeline".
Repro: v06 Eshan (T2, luck, business) → card `s-0-5`, answer 2. Fix: use the "good months" wording when `not has_best(p)`.
(b) Preview teaser (seen before payment): `diwali.py:135` (teaser lines for `luck`) always includes
"Your brightest months are marked month by month." for every time quality.
Repro: `python3 -c "import sys;sys.path[:0]=['tests','.'];import test_diwali_versions as T;[print(T.VIDS[i],T.build(i)[0]['meta']['time_quality'],T.build(i)[0]['teaser']['teasers']) for i in (5,12,19)]"`
→ v06 T2, v13 T3, v20 T3 all carry the line. Fix: pick "Your good months are marked month by month." when time quality is T2/T3.
`chk_time` now scans `payload.teaser` too and reports `T2/T3 teaser says 'brightest'` for these three.

### F7. T2/T3 best-time wording still in all 14 T2/T3 versions (`-k time` = 14 failed / 14 passed, 2026-10-26) — CONTENT + RENDER-A
`chk_time` now catches a superlative tied to a time across commas, up to 8 words, inside one sentence.
The lines it finds in every T2/T3 report: "Lean on it most for <area>, <when>." (3 per report),
"Money comes most easily through <X>, mainly <when>.", "Work that rewards ... suits you, and it shines <when>.",
and in the free preview "Your business grows best in ●●●–●●●." / "Home life glows most in ●●●–●●●." /
"There is a named month when a move suits you best." / "Your best months for family functions are marked."
Each one names a single strongest time, which spec §2 rule 10 forbids with an unsure birth time.
Repro: `python3 -c "import sys;sys.path[:0]=['tests','.'];import test_diwali_versions as T;print(T.chk_time(*T.build(2)))"` (v03 Chirag).
Fix: for T2/T3 drop the `<when>` part (or say "across your good months"). "Peak phase" (Sade Sati stage) is not a claim and is not flagged.

## Things that pass the tests but need an owner/agent decision

- **O1. Rule-B Jupiter signal differs from the spec text.** Spec §5: Jupiter passes or aspects the houses "at any
  point from this Diwali to the next". Engine (`diwali.py:648-664`) counts only the sign Jupiter holds ≥ 6 of 12
  months, documented as matching the Vikram worked example. With the spec wording 26 of 28 score sets change and the
  area order changes for 7 versions (v01, v04, v12, v16, v17, v19, v28). The test follows the engine reading; the spec
  should be updated to say "for most of the span", or the engine changed. Owner decides.
- **O2. Chart reasons outside the Why line.** R2 says the "why" line is the only place a chart reason appears, and
  the approved mock's what-lines name no planet. In the 28 versions 952 of 982 topic what-lines name a planet
  ("With Ketu in Karka, you can see past the noise…", "Saturn is exalted in Libra, so…"). Not a bracket, so lint
  passes, but it reads busier than mock3c-v2. CONTENT, if the owner wants the mock's style.
- **O3. Tag vs When contradiction (1 case).** v04 Deepika, Marriage topic 1: tag CAREFUL, "When: good time"
  (shot `version-shots/v04-Deepika-marriage-p27.png`). Every other Careful tag has a careful When.
- **O4. Practices page is the same for everyone.** `diwali_report_back.py:632` `PRACTICES` is a constant; only the
  "Why these" line changes. Spec §5 3.4 says the planets that get practices differ per buyer, and `payload.practices`
  exists but is not used on the page. RENDER-B.
- **O5. Wrapped "Good time" tile in the solid green Best style** when a T0/T1 chart has no best month
  (v14 shot). Fine by rule 10, but the green fill reads as Best. RENDER-B, minor.
- **O6. Half-year headline overstates.** 12 months p.1 "a good start, then go carefully." when only 1 of the 5 later months
  is careful (v10 shot). `half_headline` picks careful over good. RENDER-B, minor.
- **O7. "Best time" in the picture key of T2/T3 reports.** `chk_time` deliberately removes three picture-key phrases before its
  search: "Full bloom your best time", "Bright with rays: best time" and "mean a best time". They print in every T2/T3 report
  (Family, Marriage, 12-month and How-to-read cards, e.g. "Full bloom your best time for relationships"), although spec
  lines 103-104 say T2/T3 has no Best time label. They explain a symbol the buyer never sees on those reports.
  Repro: `python3 -c "import sys,re;sys.path[:0]=['tests','.'];import test_diwali_versions as T;p,h=T.build(2);print(re.findall(r'Full bloom your best time for [a-z]+',T.visible(h)))"`.
  **Owner decision:** keep the key as is, or drop the best-time row from the key when `not has_best(p)` (RENDER-A/B); if dropped, delete the exclusion in `chk_time`.
