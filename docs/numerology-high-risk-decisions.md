## Worth knowing before you decide
- **Holding back a number blocks every customer who has it.** A combination is sold only when every entry it needs is approved. If you hold back B2, B5 or B9, anyone with that Bhagyank cannot buy until it is sorted. Holding back M4 or M7 does the same for those Mulank numbers.
- **The fastest route** is A on all seven: approve as editorial writing, keep the current wording, and rely on the "belief system, not a science" line already on the cards. That is a business decision, not a factual finding. The automated checks confirm the wording is clean and consistent; they do not confirm it is correct.
- **A middle route** is B on items 1 and 2, which is only a wording change to say traditions differ, and A elsewhere.
- **"Contemporary writing" wording:** several entries say a view comes from "contemporary writing". The repository does not record which sources. Decide whether that phrasing is acceptable, or ask engineering to soften it to "some traditions".
- Approval is recorded by name and date through a reviewed PR. Nothing is approved by reading this page.
