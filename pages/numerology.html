How your two numbers are worked out

In Indian numerology, Mulank comes from your birth day and Bhagyank comes from your whole date of birth. Each is added down to a single digit. Contemporary writers read Mulank as how you go about things and Bhagyank as the direction you are headed.

Mulank example. Born on the 27th: 2 + 7 = 9, so the Mulank is 9.
Bhagyank example. Born 05-04-1988: 0+5+0+4+1+9+8+8 = 35, then 3 + 5 = 8, so the Bhagyank is 8.

Some writers keep 11 and 22 as special numbers. This report reduces them to single digits, so other readings may differ.
