Kundli milan ke 36 gun: aatho kootas ka asli matlab

Axtroshastra · Method explainer

"Kitne gun mile?" — rishtey ki baat mein yeh number aate hi hawa badal jaati hai. Par yeh 36 points aate kahan se hain, aur har point ka matlab kya hai? Ashtakoota system aath alag-alag compatibility dimensions ko measure karta hai — har ek ka apna weightage. Samajh lo, toh number se zyada uski quality dikhne lagti hai.

Aatho kootas, weightage ke order mein

  1. Varna (1 point) — work-ego compatibility. Sabse halka weightage, sabse kam decisive.
  2. Vashya (2) — mutual influence; kaun kis par sway rakhta hai.
  3. Tara (3) — nakshatra-count se bond ki strength aur stability.
  4. Yoni (4) — instinctive aur physical harmony; nakshatra-animals ki compatibility.
  5. Graha Maitri (5) — dono moon signs ke lords ki dosti; mental wavelength.
  6. Gana (6) — temperament: Deva, Manushya, Rakshasa categories ka match.
  7. Bhakoot (7) — moon signs ki relative position; emotional bond aur family growth. Kuch combinations (6-8, 2-12, 5-9) dosha maane jaate hain.
  8. Nadi (8 points — sabse heavy) — traditionally progeny aur vitality se juda. Same Nadi = dosha = 0 points.

Score ko kaise padhein

Traditional threshold 18/36 hai. 18–24 acceptable, 25–32 very good, 33+ excellent. Lekin do couples ka same score bilkul alag quality ka ho sakta hai: 20 points with Nadi intact ≠ 20 points with Nadi dosha. Isliye breakdown score se zyada important hai.

Nadi dosha — aur uska cancellation

Kyunki Nadi ke 8 points hain, Nadi dosha akela score ko heavily gira deta hai. Par yahan bhi cancellation rules hain: agar dono ki moon rashi alag ho, ya nakshatra same par pada alag, toh classical texts dosha ko cancelled maante hain. "Nadi dosha hai, rishta nahi ho sakta" sunte hi rukiye mat — pehle cancellation check karwaiye.
