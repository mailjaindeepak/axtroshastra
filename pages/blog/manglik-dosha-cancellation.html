Manglik dosha kya hai — aur kab cancel ho jaata hai

Axtroshastra · Myth-buster

"Ladki manglik hai" — yeh teen shabd Indian rishton mein jitna dar failaate hain, utna shayad hi kuch aur. Lekin classical texts padho toh picture bahut alag hai: manglik dosha common hai, conditional hai, aur bahut baar cancel ho jaata hai. Chaliye bina dar ke samajhte hain.

Manglik hota kya hai?

Jab Mars (Mangal) kundli ke kuch specific houses mein ho — 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th ya 12th — toh chart manglik kehlata hai. Yeh check lagna se bhi hota hai aur Moon se bhi. Mars in houses mein hone ka classical matlab: marriage-related matters mein Mars ki energy — intensity, friction — active rehti hai.

Kitne log manglik hote hain?

Simple probability: 12 mein se 6 houses manglik hain, do reference points se check hota hai. Roughly har teesra-chautha chart kisi na kisi definition se manglik nikalta hai. Itna common hona hi bata deta hai ki yeh koi rare shraap nahi ho sakta.

Cancellation — jo baat aksar batayi nahi jaati

Classical texts mein manglik dosha ke cancellation rules utne hi clearly likhe hain jitna dosha khud: Mars apne own sign (Aries, Scorpio) mein ho toh dosha significantly weak; Mars exalted (Capricorn) ho toh bhi; Jupiter ka Mars ke saath hona ya aspect karna soften karta hai; aur kuch traditions mein age ke saath dosha ka effect khatam maana jaata hai. Aur sabse practical rule: manglik-manglik matching mein dosha neutral ho jaata hai — dono charts mein Mars active hai toh balance ban jaata hai.

Toh karna kya chahiye?

Pehle proper check karwaiye — sirf "manglik hai" nahi, balki kahan se, kaunse house mein, aur cancellation apply hota hai ya nahi. Aadha dar adhoori jaankari se aata hai. Report mein yeh teeno cheezein clearly likhi honi chahiye, taaki family decision facts par ho, dar par nahi.
