Birth time nahi pata? Kundli phir bhi banti hai

Axtroshastra · Method explainer

"Exact time toh nahi pata, bas itna yaad hai ki subah ka time tha." Yeh line har teesre-chauthe ghar mein sunayi deti hai — purane zamane mein birth time minute-level note hota hi nahi tha. Toh kya bina exact time ke kundli banana bekaar hai? Bilkul nahi — classical Jyotish ke paas iske liye ek proper, time-tested method hai.

Time se kya-kya badalta hai — honestly

Teen cheezein alag speed se change hoti hain. Lagna (ascendant) har ~2 ghante mein sign badal leta hai — isliye exact time ke bina lagna unreliable ho jaata hai. Moon ek nakshatra mein zyaadatar poora din rehta hai — isliye Moon-based calculations din bhar stable rehti hain. Aur dasha timeline Moon ki position se nikalti hai, toh woh thodi shift hoti hai par tootti nahi — windows bas wider ho jaati hain.

Chandra Lagna: Moon ko hi lagna maan lo

Classical Parashari texts ka standard solution: jab lagna reliable na ho, Moon sign ko reference point banakar poora chart padho. Ise Chandra Lagna kehte hain. Houses Moon se count hote hain — 7th from Moon marriage ke liye, 10th from Moon career ke liye. Yeh koi jugaad nahi, poori tarah traditional method hai jo purane astrologers routinely use karte the.

Aur kundli milan? Wahan toh time almost matter hi nahi karta

36-guna Ashtakoota matching ke saare kootas Moon ki rashi aur nakshatra se nikalte hain. Matlab: milan ke liye date aur place hi kaafi hai — exact time ho toh pada-level refinement milta hai, par core score date se ban jaata hai. Jo website time ke bina milan se mana kare ya chupchaap 12:00 assume kar le, woh method nahi jaanti.

Rectification — time waapas nikaalne ka tareeka

Advanced method bhi hai: aapki zindagi ke dated events (shaadi, job change, bade shifts) ko alag-alag possible birth times ke against test karke best-fit time band nikaala ja sakta hai. Ise birth time rectification kehte hain — aur yeh approximate-time charts ko precise bana deta hai.
