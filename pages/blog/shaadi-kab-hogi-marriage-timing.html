Shaadi kab hogi? Kundli se marriage timing kaise nikalti hai

Axtroshastra · Method explainer

"Shaadi kab hogi?" — Indian astrology ka sabse zyada poocha jaane wala sawaal. Aur sabse zyada gol-mol jawab bhi isi ka milta hai: "jaldi hi ho jayegi", "thoda Shani ka chakkar hai". Sach yeh hai ki classical Jyotish mein marriage timing ka ek proper, step-by-step method hai — jo koi bhi seekh sakta hai. Aaj wahi method samjhaate hain.

Step 1: Chart ke marriage factors pehchano

Har kundli mein kuch specific factors marriage ko govern karte hain: 7th house (partnership ka ghar), 7th house ka lord (woh planet jo us sign ka swami hai), Venus (sabhi charts mein marriage ka karaka), aur female charts mein additionally Jupiter. In factors ki position aur strength aapke marriage pattern ki foundation hai.

Step 2: Dasha timeline — kab kaunsa planet active hai

Vimshottari dasha system aapki zindagi ko planetary periods mein baant deta hai — har mahadasha ke andar antardashas. Classical rule simple hai: marriage typically tab trigger hota hai jab chal rahe dasha ya antardasha ka lord aapke 7th house ya uske lord se connected ho. Matlab: agar aapka 7th lord Jupiter hai, toh Jupiter ki antardasha aapke strongest periods mein se ek hogi.

Step 3: Transit confirmation — Jupiter ka role

Dasha batata hai ki darwaza kab khul sakta hai; transit batata hai ki us waqt hawa kis taraf hai. Sabse important transit Jupiter ka hai — jab Jupiter aapke lagna ya Moon se 1st, 2nd, 7th ya 11th house se guzarta hai, marriage yog activate hota hai. Jupiter ka cycle ~12 saal ka hai, isliye har decade mein yeh windows zaroor aati hain. Saturn ka 7th house transit delay dikha sakta hai — denial nahi, delay.

Windows, dates nahi — yeh important kyun hai

Dasha aur transit milkar time windows dete hain — mahino se saalon tak ke periods jab probability high hoti hai. Koi bhi honest method exact date nahi de sakta, kyunki ±15 minute ka birth time difference bhi calculations ko shift kar deta hai. Jo astrologer exact date ya "pakka ho jayega" bole, woh method se nahi, confidence se bol raha hai.
