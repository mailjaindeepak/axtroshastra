Q&A

Frequently asked questions

What score counts as compatible?
The traditional threshold is 18/36. 18–24 is workable, 25–32 is strong, 33+ is excellent. But the number matters less than which areas are low — a shortfall in one area isn't the same as another. Your report explains that difference clearly instead of leaving you with just a number.
There's a Nadi dosha — is that a dealbreaker?
Usually not. Nadi dosha has classical cancellation rules — different signs, different star-quarters, and a few other conditions. Your report shows the dosha along with its cancellation check, so you get the full picture instead of just a one-line verdict.
Why might an astrologer's score differ?
Different calculation settings and manual shortcuts. We use standard classical tables and show the detail behind every part of the score — so you can get it checked line by line by anyone you trust.
We don't know our birth times. Will it work?
Yes — the match is Moon-based, and that stays stable through the day (barring rare edge cases). An exact time refines a couple of finer checks, but for your core score, the date and place are enough.
When do we get the report?

Are our details safe?
