Why it works

Why astrology, computed, can be trusted

"AI" here doesn't mean a black box guessing your future. It means the exact opposite: a deterministic engine that applies classical Jyotish rules to real planetary data — so the answer comes from your chart, not from someone's opinion that day.

It is deterministic — same chart, same answer, every time.

Give the engine the same birth details and it returns the identical result, today or a year from now. Two different people asking about the same chart get the same reading. That reproducibility is what a human astrologer, on a busy day or a good mood, cannot promise.

The planetary positions are pure science.

We compute your chart from NASA-grade planetary data — the same JPL ephemeris professional observatories use — with the sidereal zodiac, Lahiri ayanamsa and whole-sign houses. There is zero manual calculation and zero room for human arithmetic error.

Classical rules, encoded — not invented.

Dashas, transits, house lords, the 36-guna Ashtakoota tables, Manglik checks and their cancellation rules — the same principles a good astrologer uses, written into software exactly as the classical texts lay them out. Nothing is made up by a machine.

Every answer shows its reasoning.

Each report names the dasha, the rule and the transit behind its conclusion. You can take any line to any astrologer and have it verified against the shastra. Transparency is the whole point — a reading you can check is a reading you can trust.

Why we built it

India's heritage deserves better than spam calls and scare tactics.

For too many people, astrology has become a place to be frightened, flattered or fleeced — vague predictions, expensive "remedies", and follow-up calls that never stop. We think that's a betrayal of a genuinely rich tradition.

Our intent is simple: give ordinary people honest, unbiased guidance drawn from India's centuries-old astronomical heritage, delivered by calculation instead of sales pressure. We tell you what the chart actually indicates — windows and probabilities, not guarantees — whether the news is good or not. No one is trying to sell you a gemstone at the end. That is the whole initiative: making one of India's oldest sciences trustworthy, affordable and accessible again.

Our promises

What we promise — and what we don't

We don't guarantee. We give probabilities.

Anyone who says something is "pakka" is lying. A chart shows windows and indications; we report them honestly, favourable or not.

We never inflate scores.

If a milan score is low, it shows low — with the cancellation rules and the practical meaning. A feel-good report doesn't help anyone.

Your details are never sold.

Birth details are used only for your report. No spam calls, no astrologer follow-ups, no data selling — common in this industry, and something we simply don't do.
