QUESTIONS

Frequently asked

What does this IQ test measure?
It measures four cognitive areas: pattern recognition, logical reasoning, verbal-analogical thinking, and classification ability. These are core dimensions of analytical intelligence.
How accurate is the IQ score?
The test uses a calibrated scoring model with age-based norming, similar to standard psychometric assessments. It gives a reliable estimate of your analytical reasoning ability. It is not a professional assessment tool.
What is in the paid report?
Your overall IQ score, a detailed breakdown of all 4 categories, your cognitive strengths, growth areas with specific tips, your learning style, and personalised recommendations. 11 pages, delivered to your WhatsApp as a PDF.
How long does the test take?
15 minutes maximum. Most people finish in 10-12 minutes. The timer runs for the full 15 minutes, and the test auto-submits when time is up.
Can I retake the test?
Yes, you can take the test again anytime. Each attempt generates a new report.
Is my data safe?
Yes. We do not share your answers or scores with anyone. Your report is private and delivered only to your WhatsApp number.
